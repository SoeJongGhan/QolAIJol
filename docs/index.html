Same model as the app

YOLO26n fine-tuned by the team on 6,375 pedestrian-view SANPO frames, 9 classes. Exported to ONNX (9.8 MB) and run here with ONNX Runtime Web on the CPU. Nothing is uploaded.

Same warning rules

  • confidence ≥ 0.50 and seen in 2 rounds
  • vehicle, cyclist: HIGH · person, animal: MEDIUM
  • pole, obstacle: LOW, or MEDIUM when ahead
  • one warning at a time, 2.5 s gap, 10 s repeat cooldown
  • stairs, traffic light, crosswalk: never spoken

Limits

A research prototype, not a mobility aid. On the SANPO test set the detector finds about 6 in 10 objects closer than 2 m. Keep using a cane or guide dog. Night, rain and indoor scenes are untested.
